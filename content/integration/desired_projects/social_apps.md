Enables viral social applications that onboard non-DeFi users while protecting social graphs, content, and user interactions from public surveillance.

## Functionality

- Support private social trading (follow traders, copy trades, private leaderboards with ZK proofs)
- Enable encrypted social networks (messaging, group chats, shielded social graphs, anonymous posts with optional reputation)
- Provide creator monetization (private subscriptions, shielded content access tokens, anonymous tipping)
- Support social tokens (private community tokens, shielded creator content access)
- Enable private prediction markets (forecast events without revealing bets)
- Support anonymous forums & communities (token-gated access, reputation without identity)

## Usability

- Web2-level UX with Web3 privacy
- Gasless transactions (subsidized or batched)
- Fast, real-time interactions
- Mobile-first design
- Social recovery for key management

## Performance

- Scalability for social-scale traffic (1000s TPS)

## Design Constraints (Privacy)

- Protect social graphs (connections and followers remain private)
- Shield financial activity (purchases, tips, trades not exposed)
- Prevent surveillance (platform cannot build user dossiers)
- Enable authentic expression (post without permanent public record)
- Protect influencers (high-value accounts not targeted based on earnings)

## Demand Validation

**Potential Users:** Social media users, content creators, traders, community members, prediction market participants

**Use Cases:**
- Private social trading and copy trading
- Anonymous community participation
- Creator monetization without exposing earnings
- Private messaging and social networking
- Token-gated communities without revealing holdings
- Anonymous prediction markets and reputation building

## Possible Implementation

- **Encrypted messaging**: End-to-end encrypted communications
- **Private social graph**: Connections not stored publicly
- **Anonymous reputation**: Prove reputation without revealing identity via ZK
- **Shielded micropayments**: Tips and subscriptions without exposure
- **Private content access**: Token-gated content with hidden ownership
- **Private referral programs**: Prove referrals via ZK
- **Anonymous leaderboards**: Achievements without identity exposure

## Technical Validation

**Risks & Challenges:**
- Scalability for social-scale traffic (1000s TPS)
- UX complexity of key management
- Preventing Sybil attacks without KYC
- Content moderation without centralized visibility
- Building initial network effects (cold start problem)
- Balancing privacy with discoverability
- Preventing abuse (spam, harassment) while preserving anonymity
- Mechanism design to incentivize genuine engagement
- Integration with existing social platforms for onboarding
